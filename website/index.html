Homage to Savile Row

English tailoring convention, carried out in New Haven without the theatre.

Bespoke means the pattern starts with you, not with a size. Your measurements become a pattern drafted for you alone. A test garment, the toile, is cut from it and fitted on you before your cloth is touched.

Every garment we make is made fully by hand. There is no made-to-measure option and no rail of finished suits: the house does one thing.

What bespoke means here

How a commission works

Three or four visits, eight to twelve weeks.

Most people hesitate over bespoke because they don't know how long it takes or how often they'll need to come in. This is the whole of it.

  1. Week 0

    Consultation

    Conversation, measurements, cloth.

    You attend

  2. About week 3

    Toile fitting

    A test garment, fitted and corrected on you.

    You attend

  3. Weeks 4–8

    Making

    Cut from your cloth and made by hand.

    In the workshop

  4. About week 8

    Final fitting

    The garment on you; last marks made.

    You attend

  5. Weeks 8–12

    Collection

    Pressed, finished, and yours.

    You attend
