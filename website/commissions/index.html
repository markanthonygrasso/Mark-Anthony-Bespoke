Formal and weddings

Dinner suits, morning dress, white tie.

For the groom, and for the wedding party if they want to match. A wedding has a fixed date, so book the consultation at least twelve weeks before it; more is better.

Why people commission

Usually, something has changed.

A new role. A wedding. Having lost weight and owning nothing that fits. A deliberate change in how you want to present yourself. Or simply wanting, once, to have something made properly.

Any of those is a good reason to start. The consultation is where we work out what to make first.

Price

What it costs.

Price depends on the garment and the cloth. You'll have the full figure at the consultation, before anything is ordered.

The first commission in each kind of garment (suit, jacket, trousers, waistcoat, overcoat) includes a toile. Later commissions of the same kind start from your existing pattern and don't need one.
