The Craft

What bespoke means here.

The word gets used loosely. Here it means three things: a pattern drafted for you alone, a toile fitted before your cloth is cut, and a garment made entirely by hand.

Three things that make it bespoke

One

Your own pattern

Drafted from your measurements, not adjusted from a stock size. It is kept, so the next garment of the same kind starts from it.

Two

A toile first

A test garment in plain cloth, fitted on you, so corrections are made to the pattern rather than to your cloth.

Three

Made fully by hand

Every garment, every time. There is no part-handmade tier and no made-to-measure.

The timeline

From first conversation to collection.

A first commission is three or four visits over eight to twelve weeks. Between visits the work is in the workshop and there's nothing for you to do.

You attend In the workshop
  1. 1

    Week 0 · 90 minutes

    Consultation

    We talk about what the commission is for and how you'll wear it, take your measurements and photographs, and go through the cloth books together. Most clients settle the cloth at this sitting.

    You attend

    About three weeks until your next visit

  2. 2

    Weeks 1–3

    Pattern and toile

    Your pattern is drafted from your measurements. A toile, a test garment in plain cloth, is cut from it.

    In the workshop — nothing for you to do

  3. 3

    About week 3 · 45 minutes

    Toile fitting

    You try the toile on. We look at the slope of the shoulders, the balance front to back, where the cloth pulls and where it pools, and mark every change. The pattern is corrected before anything is cut from your cloth.

    You attend

    About five weeks until your next visit

  4. 4

    Weeks 4–8

    Making

    Your cloth is cut to the corrected pattern and the garment is made entirely by hand.

    In the workshop — nothing for you to do

  5. 5

    About week 8 · 45 minutes

    Final fitting

    The garment in your cloth, on you for the first time. Last adjustments are marked here.

    You attend

    Up to four weeks until your next visit

  6. 6

    Weeks 8–12 · 30 minutes

    Collection

    Final adjustments made, the garment pressed, and it goes home with you.

    You attend

Returning for another garment of the same kind? Your pattern already exists, so there's no toile: three visits rather than four.
