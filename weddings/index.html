Weddings

Your wedding suit, made for you.

The suit you’ll see in photographs for the rest of your life should be cut for you alone.

Photograph to come
Why bespoke

Made to fit on the day, and for years after.

A rented or off-the-rack suit is made to fit a size. A bespoke suit is cut from your own pattern, fitted on you, and made entirely by hand, so it sits properly in every photograph and every embrace.

It is also yours to keep and wear again: at anniversaries, at other people’s weddings, and on any day that deserves it.

Timing

Begin three to six months before.

The full bespoke process takes at least twelve weeks. Starting three to six months before the wedding leaves room to enjoy it, and to settle the details without hurry.

If your date is closer, rush options are available. Ask at your consultation.

The Process

A minimum of three fittings.

  1. I

    Consultation

    Conversation, cloth, measurements.

  2. II

    Toile fitting

    Your test garment, fitted and corrected on you.

  3. III

    Making

    Cut from your cloth and made by hand. A suit takes over fifty hours.

  4. IV

    Final fitting

    Your finished garment, on you for the first time.

  5. V

    Collection

    Final refinements made, pressed, and yours.
