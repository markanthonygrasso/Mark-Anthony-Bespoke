The Standard

Savile Row tradition, honored in New Haven.

Bespoke means the pattern starts with you, not with a size. Your measurements become a pattern of your own. Your cloth isn’t cut until your toile, a test garment, has been fitted.

Every garment we make is made fully by hand. There is no made-to-measure option and no rack of finished suits. The house does one thing, and does it properly.

The Bespoke Experience

The Process

A minimum of three fittings. Ten to twelve weeks.

  1. I

    Consultation

    Conversation, cloth, measurements.

  2. II

    Toile fitting

    Your test garment, fitted and corrected on you.

  3. III

    Making

    Cut from your cloth and made by hand. A suit takes over fifty hours.

  4. IV

    Final fitting

    Your finished garment, on you for the first time.

  5. V

    Collection

    Final refinements made, pressed, and yours.
